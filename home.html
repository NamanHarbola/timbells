Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long does an order take to make?

Most pieces are handmade and shipped within 3–5 days of confirming your order. Larger custom pieces may take a little longer — we'll always tell you upfront.

Can I choose my own colours?

Yes! Almost every product can be made in your choice of colours. Just mention it when you DM or WhatsApp us.

Do you ship pan-India?

Yes, we ship across India. Delivery usually takes 3–7 days depending on your location after the order is shipped.

How do I place a custom order?

Head to our Custom Order page, or simply DM/WhatsApp us your idea, reference image, or character — we'll confirm design and price before starting.

What if I'm not happy with my order?

Since every piece is handmade to order, we work closely with you on design and colour before starting to make sure there are no surprises. Reach out to us directly if anything's off.
